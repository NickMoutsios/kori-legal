TeleKori — Support

Last updated 10 October 2026

TeleKori lets you run simple vision checks on yourself and follow changes over time. It does not diagnose or treat eye conditions and is not a substitute for an examination by a qualified eye doctor.

Contact us

Email kori.info@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the version of the app, which you will find in your device's app settings.

Do not send photographs of your eye, or any health information, by email. We do not need them to help you, and email is not a secure way to send them.

Common questions

QuestionAnswer
Does my data leave my phone? No. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
Can TeleKori tell me what is wrong with my eyes? No. It records what you see and report. Only a qualified eye doctor can examine your eyes and explain what your results mean.
Where are my eye photos and videos saved? In a Kori folder inside your device's Download area, kept out of your photo gallery on purpose. They stay there after you uninstall the app, so delete the folder yourself if you want them gone.
How do I delete my data? Delete a profile in the app to remove its results. Uninstalling removes the app's own data; see the row above for captured photos and videos.
My results changed, or something worries me. See an eye care professional. TeleKori cannot answer that for you.

Reporting a problem

If the app crashes or behaves unexpectedly, tell us what you were doing when it happened, your device model, and the app version. There is no automatic crash reporting, so a problem you do not tell us about is one we will not see.
