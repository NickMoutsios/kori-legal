TeleKori — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 10 October 2026

Everything you enter in TeleKori stays on your device. We do not run a server for it, we cannot see it, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Who we are

TeleKori is provided by KORI EYES TECH SINGLE MEMBER P.C., Ksenokratous 69, 12137 Athens, Greece ("we", "us").

For any question about this policy, contact kori.info@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores, and where

All of the following is kept in the app's own storage on your device:

WhatDetails
Profile A name, gender, and optionally an age. You choose what to enter; a name can be anything you like.
Results of your checks The outcome of each vision check you run — Amsler grid, colour vision, contrast, visual acuity, red colour — with the date and time.
Eye photos and videos Images and video of the eye you capture with the camera, including sound if you record video.

Photos and videos of your eye are biometric data. The app saves them to a folder of its own and deliberately keeps them out of your photo gallery, so they are not picked up by gallery apps or photo backup services.

One thing to know: unlike the rest of the app's data, that folder is in your device's public Download area, so it is not removed when you uninstall the app. Delete the folder yourself if you want those files gone.

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
CameraTo photograph or film the eye. Only while you are capturing.
MicrophoneSound is recorded as part of an eye video. Only while a video is recording.
StorageTo save captures and results on the device.

You can refuse or withdraw any of these in your device settings. The rest of the app keeps working; you simply cannot capture photos or video.

4. Our role

We do not collect your data. The app writes it to your device and we never receive it, so there is nothing for us to store, analyse, sell or hand over. If you use TeleKori for yourself or your family, your use is a private matter and this policy describes a product, not a service that processes your data.

If you use the app to record checks for other people, then you — not we — decide why and how their information is handled, and any legal duties that come with that are yours.

5. Who we share data with

Nobody. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting that carries your data, and no third-party software that collects anything. Because your information never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to share.

If you choose to show or send your results to an eye care professional, that is your own action and outside this policy.

6. How long it is kept

For as long as you keep it. We set no retention period, because we hold nothing. Deleting a profile removes its results from the app. Uninstalling removes the app's own data — but see the note in section 2 about captured photos and videos.

A backup made by your phone or your computer may include the app's data. That backup is under your control, not ours.

7. Security

Data sits in the app's private storage area, which the operating system keeps separate from other apps, and eye captures are kept out of your shared gallery on purpose. Protection therefore rests mainly on your device: use a screen lock, keep the operating system up to date, and think before handing the phone to someone who should not see the contents.

8. Children

TeleKori is not designed for or directed at children, and we ask that it is not used by children on their own.

9. Your rights

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies, you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability.

In this version you exercise all of them directly on the device: the data is yours, visible in the app, editable in the app, and removable by deleting the profile or the app. We cannot retrieve, correct or delete it for you, because we never receive it.

You may also complain to your national data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how information is handled — in particular saving your results to our servers — we will update this policy, raise its version number, and ask for your agreement before any such feature is switched on. We will not begin sending data off your device under this version of the policy.
